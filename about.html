Hi, I’m Jessica.

I’m studying Interactive Arts and Technology at Simon Fraser University, exploring the space where visual design meets interaction.

My work moves between interface design, illustration, and motion. I enjoy giving an idea a distinct personality, then working through the details that make it understandable and usable.

Sometimes that means building a game from my own drawings. Sometimes it means making a large set of app screens feel consistent, or adding just enough movement to an existing illustration. Across these projects, I’m interested in how small visual decisions shape the whole experience.
