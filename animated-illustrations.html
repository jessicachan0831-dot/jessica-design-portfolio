Animated Illustrations – Studio Motion Work

A selection of short animated illustrations created during my work at an art studio. The project involved transforming existing static artwork into subtle, loopable motion assets that could later be incorporated into larger animated sequences and video productions.

Motion Design; Animated Illustration

Layer Preparation, Subtle Motion

Adobe Photoshop

Professional Studio Work

Project Overview & My Role

The original illustrations were provided by the studio. My role was to prepare and animate selected visual elements in Adobe Photoshop while preserving the style, composition, and atmosphere of the original artwork.

I organized visual elements into separate layers and introduced subtle motion to elements such as birds, environmental details, and atmospheric effects. The goal was not to completely redesign the illustrations, but to add enough movement to make each image feel more alive while still remaining visually consistent with the original artwork.

Process

I created these animations using Photoshop’s timeline tools. For each illustration, I first analyzed which parts of the image could move naturally and which elements should remain stable in order to preserve the original composition and mood.

I then separated the artwork into foreground, middle-ground, and background layers. When moving elements were cut out from the original image, this often revealed transparent gaps behind them, so I repainted or extended the hidden areas to ensure the animation remained visually complete and did not expose empty spaces.

After preparing the layers, I used the timeline to animate position, timing, and subtle directional movement. For each piece, I considered how the scene could feel more vivid through changes in depth, angle, scale, and motion rhythm. Rather than adding excessive movement, I focused on creating restrained motion that made each illustration feel more alive while still remaining consistent with the original artwork.

Design Considerations

Because the original artwork was created by other artists, it was important that the animation supported rather than changed their visual intentions. The timing, direction, and amount of movement were designed to remain consistent with the mood and composition of each illustration.

Outcome

The completed motion assets transformed static illustrations into short, atmospheric animations while retaining the original artists’ visual style. The project strengthened my ability to work carefully with existing creative assets and to produce motion that supports a larger production workflow.
