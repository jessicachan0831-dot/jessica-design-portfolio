Hi, I'm
Jessica Chan

An graphic designer passionate about creating playful and visually cohesive digital experiences.

Currently pursuing a degree in Interactive Arts and Technology at Simon Fraser University, I concentrate on design & development for web and mobile. My work blends UI design, illustration, and interactive development, aiming to craft experiences that feel both delightful and intuitive.

I enjoy designing visuals that tell stories—whether through interactive games, motion graphics, or immersive web experiences. My portfolio showcases projects where I combined hand-drawn aesthetics with functional UI to enhance user engagement, reflecting my approach to design as both creative and user-focused.
