Animated Illustrations

Working with the studio’s artwork

The illustrations already had their own atmosphere and visual balance. My task was to add movement that supported those qualities. I looked for elements that could move naturally — birds, environmental details, and layers of landscape — while keeping the larger composition stable.

The original artwork was provided by the studio and created by other artists. The work shown here focuses on my layer preparation and animation contribution.

Layer preparation and animation

I separated the original images into foreground, middle-ground, and background layers. Moving those layers exposed gaps, so I repainted or extended the hidden areas before animating. This preparation kept the image complete throughout each loop.

In Photoshop’s timeline, I adjusted position, scale, timing, and direction. Different movement rates created depth, while restrained timing kept the animation close to the mood of the illustration.

Reflection

The finished loops preserve the original artists’ visual style while adding atmosphere and depth. The process strengthened my ability to work within an existing creative direction, prepare assets carefully, and use small changes in timing to make an image feel alive.
