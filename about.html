A bit of background

I study Interactive Arts and Technology at Simon Fraser University. My projects combine graphic design, illustration, interfaces, and animation.

I like working with drawings as well as screens. For Dessert Simulator, I illustrated the characters and ingredients and wrote the game in Java. For UniBazaar, I worked with a teammate and focused on the interface layouts and visual system.

I’ve also worked with existing artwork at an art studio, separating images into layers and animating them in Photoshop. That work involved keeping the original composition intact while adding movement.
